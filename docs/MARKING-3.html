As per the project description, Project 3 focuses on the clients: their communication with the server
and their Human Computer Interface (HCI). The marking for this part of the project reﬂects this
focus:
- 20% is for customer and casino/table management
- 35% of the marks are for distributed game play
- 25% for HCI design and layout
- 20% is for project design and management
As for Project 1, the team will walk the marker through the typical scenario where three players
enter the casino, create or access their accounts to get some chips, and then play one hand of 7-
card Stud and one hand of Texas Hold'em. The reason we chose these two particular games is
because they both have face-up cards and the use of the face-up cards is different. Because the
games have a large number of betting rounds (ﬁve for 7-card Stud and four for Hold'em) we ask
that you provide a mechanism to skip betting rounds (e.g. everyone is allowed to pass). The ﬁrst
round of betting typically has special rules so it cannot be skipped, and your program should make
sure there is at least one more betting round, but we really need to limit the time that each demo
requires.
For demonstration purposes, you may have more than one player on a computer, but there must be
at least two computers to show that the client(s) and server can communicate over real interfaces.
Also, remember that the exciting part of managing multiple computers is that clients can come and
go and send messages at any time.
