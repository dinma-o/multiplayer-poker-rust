Project 1 Marking Template

As per the project description, Project 1 focuses on the server: managing the players, gameplay, and ranking hands. The marking for this part of the project reflects this focus:

- 20% of the marks are for the client database and its management
- 35% of the marks are for correctly playing the game
- 20% is for ranking the hands to determine the winner
- 20% is for project design and management
- 5% is for documentation and unit tests.

Demonstration

For the functional demonstration, the team will walk the marker through the typical scenario where three players enter the casino, create or access their accounts to get some chips, and play two hands of Draw Poker.
